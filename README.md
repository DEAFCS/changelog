# DEAFCS changelog

DEAFCS patch notes: new features, improvements and fixes, newest first. They also appear on the DEAFCS Changelog page (Info, then Changelog).

## Patch Notes | November 23, 2026

### New

- **Matches:** A finished 5v5 match shows its MVP under the score.
- **Players:** A sortable Level column shows each player's Career level.
- **Career:** KZ Legend, KZ Master, KZ Expert and KZ Challenger now pay Career XP and Coins (100 to 400 Coins per title, 550 XP and 1,000 Coins for all four, once per player per title).
- **Career:** Players who already held KZ titles receive the same Career XP and Coins once.

### Improved

- **Leaderboard:** An info icon on Player of the Month explains how the winner is chosen.
- **Matches:** Community GOTV waits 30 seconds after the last match's stats are ready before switching to another match.
- **Highlights:** Administrators can set a daily window for rendering highlight clips in App Settings, so live matches and streams keep the GPU. It is off by default.
- **Matches:** Community GOTV buttons explain their status on hover, keyboard focus or tap, without a separate info icon.

### Fixed

- **Awards:** Automatic leaver and no-show bans no longer stop a player from becoming Player of the Month.
- **Matches:** A finished match shows its end time once instead of twice.
- **Leaderboard:** A first visit opens on the current Season and Competitive 5v5, and the heading always names the Season shown.
- **Leaderboard:** A first visit on a slow connection no longer jumps back to All Time after a reload.

## Patch Notes | November 22, 2026

### New

- **Leaderboard:** The Season ELO Top 3 shows each place's Season reward (Coins, items and Season medal) for every mode.

### Improved

- **Profiles:** Account Settings now has your Roster Image, with a short description of where it shows.
- **Languages:** about 1,000 more texts are translated in Arabic, Danish, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Swedish, Turkish and Chinese.
- **Career:** Stacks are now called Coins everywhere on the site.
- **Leaderboard:** Wingman and Duel Season rewards show their illustrated 2v2 and 1v1 medals.
- **Leaderboard:** Every ranking shows the same Top 3 cards, with the player and the stat on one row.
- **Leaderboard:** On phones the Top 3 are three full-width rows, first place on top.

### Fixed

- **Profiles:** Roster Image no longer shows your avatar when you have not uploaded one.
- **Career:** Titles you own still show how you earned them in the Locker.
- **Languages:** party size, party seats and server update times show their numbers in every language, and broken award texts are repaired.
- **Leaderboard:** The side panel moves under the leaderboard on narrower windows or when zoomed in, and back when there is room.
- **Leaderboard:** Top 3 player names stay readable on smaller windows and when zoomed in.
- **Leaderboard:** Season Top 3 cards show ELO next to the player name whenever the card has room.

### New

## Patch Notes | November 20, 2026

### New

- **Matches:** Verified Users can start a delayed GOTV broadcast from the match menu and follow its progress next to the Spectate and GOTV buttons.
- **Matches:** When a match levels you up, the rewards card shows your new level, the Stacks earned and the new Locker items, with Equip.

### Improved

- **Matches:** a Community GOTV broadcast now ends by itself 5 minutes after its match's stats are ready, unless it is moved to another match.
- **Chat:** Photos and videos you send no longer carry your location or camera details.
- **Matches:** Replay and utility radars use Valve's own map calibration, so positions line up more precisely.

### Fixed

- **Matches:** Verified Users now see Start Stream and the stream's loading and ready status beside Spectate and GOTV.
- **Matches:** A map whose server restarted mid-game keeps the stats of its most complete demo.
- **Matches:** A map whose server restarted mid-game keeps the aim, utility and accuracy stats of all its demo recordings, on the right rounds.
- **Matches:** Unused utility on death now shows a dash instead of a misleading $0 when there is no data.
- **Matches:** Map Analysis now uses the new map radars, so kill and death markers line up with the 2D replay (Ancient fixed).
- **Teams:** The roster photo viewer stays on the same player when the roster changes.
