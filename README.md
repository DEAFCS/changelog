# DEAFCS changelog

DEAFCS patch notes: new features, improvements and fixes, newest first. They also appear on the DEAFCS Changelog page (Info, then Changelog).

## Patch Notes | November 13, 2026

### Improved

- **Leaderboard:** Podium and table numbers roll smoothly when you switch category or filters.
- **Search:** Ctrl+K shows teams first, with an exact tag match at the top.
- **Alerts:** long alert messages wrap on phones and keep their line breaks.
- **System Alerts:** links in alerts open safely, and links to DEAFCS stay in the app.

### Fixed

- **Leaderboard:** Large pages (100 players) no longer lag when switching category.
- **Matches:** T and CT stats such as ADR now count every round played on that side, not only rounds with a kill or death.
- **Servers:** Connect on a public server now joins it instead of only opening CS2.
- **Matchmaking:** a party can only queue when every member meets the queue's role requirement.
- **Alerts:** a system alert you dismissed shows again when staff put it back up.

## Patch Notes | November 12, 2026

### Improved

- **Leaderboard:** Player of the Month, Last 7 Days and the selected player now sit in a sidebar on the right, and the board keeps its full width.
- **Leaderboard:** A selected player's rank on every board shows right away, without an extra click.
- **Leaderboard:** The Role filter is gone from leaderboards; roles still show on profiles and match pages.
- **Leaderboard:** A player's rank on every board shows almost at once, and the rank bars grow in when they load.
- **Leaderboard:** Switching between players in the side panel now animates smoothly.
- **Matchmaking:** Changing your region between EU and SA now asks you to confirm first.
- **Matches:** Pages with many stats, like match pages and the leaderboard, open faster.
- **Matches:** Match pages load their round history faster.
- **Leaderboard:** Switching between categories is smooth, without the board blanking or loading twice.

### Fixed

- **Seasons:** A season whose medals were awarded keeps its dates, and a season with rated matches can't be deleted by mistake.
- **Leaderboard:** A player's rank on every board now follows the Min. matches filter, so it matches the leaderboard.
- **Leaderboard:** Tied players now show the same rank in the side panel as on the leaderboard.
- **Matches:** In a best-of-3 or best-of-5, picking a map in a player's stats now shows that map's numbers instead of the whole series.
- **Matches:** In a best-of-3 or best-of-5, the Utility tab now shows the map you picked.
- **Matches:** Buy types now use the money a team started the round with.

## Patch Notes | November 11, 2026

### New

- **Matches:** Watch lists live main broadcasts first, with both teams.

### Improved

- **Matches:** Extra stream links are now buttons with the platform icon in the match bar.
- **Matches:** Players and coaches can watch their match's delayed TV broadcast and Twitch main broadcast.
- **Matches:** Watch now updates matches, streams and viewer counts by itself, no refresh needed.
- **Matches:** Player Twitch streams now show as POV links in the match bar, and the big video area is only used for the official broadcast.

### Fixed

- **Matches:** The GOTV delay can no longer be changed by non-admins while a match is running.
- **Matches:** The live stream scoreboard shows workshop maps by their name, such as Mini Dust 2, instead of a number.
- **Servers:** Connect on Public Servers now opens CS2 straight away, without the connection guide.
- **Servers:** Connect on Public Servers now only opens CS2; copying the address stays on the Copy button.

### New

- **Leaderboard:** click a player to see their ranks and stats beside the board, with Player of the Month shown by default.
- **Support:** you can paste a screenshot with Ctrl+V when writing a support request or reply.

- **Tournaments:** organizers can add a Twitch channel, shown as a Twitch button on the tournament page once it starts.
- **News:** new articles can be announced automatically in the DEAFCS Discord.

- **Matches:** staff can show a Twitch channel as the match's main broadcast or as a Twitch button in the match bar.

### Improved

- **Watch:** players live on Twitch are now shown on the Watch page.
- **Changelog:** patch notes are grouped into fewer, clearer sections.
- **Profiles:** when you are signed in, DEAFCS opens on your profile stats.
- **Leaderboards:** the Last 7 Days records are now on the Leaderboard, under Player of the Month.
- **Matchmaking:** Your Schedule on Play now shows your later matches, solo and free-agent sign-ups, and a Show more button.
- **Profiles:** Twitch channels are now linked by signing in with Twitch, so only the real owner can add one.

- **Servers:** public servers are shown as compact rows with a map picture, mode, Full status and player count, on the Public Servers page and on Play.

- **Servers:** the Public Servers page is back to the large server cards, sorted by players.
- **Servers:** Connect explains how to join with the CS2 console if the game does not open, with a Copy button.
- **Watch:** live match cards count each stream once.
- **Watch:** players streaming their live DEAFCS match show on Watch whatever they stream.

### Fixed

- **Matchmaking:** Your Schedule on Play now shows your tournament check-in status instead of always saying Registered.
- **Watch:** sections on the Watch and Play pages are separated by a thin line again.
- **Streams:** only administrators get direct GOTV access; streamers and tournament organizers broadcast delayed TV within their matches.

- **Matches:** DEAFCS Pro matches now show the Pro badge in match lists and on profiles.

## Patch Notes | November 8, 2026

### New

- **Teams:** Teams can pick a country, shown as a flag beside the team name on team pages, match pages and tournament team lists.
- **Tournaments:** organizers can choose Random or By ELO seeding and whether check-in needs an admin review.
- **Tournaments:** Organizers can set Freeze Time next to Round Restart Delay.
- **Tournaments:** organizers can seed a tournament by team ELO instead of at random.
- **Tournaments:** organizers choose whether check-in needs an admin review; without it the bracket is drawn automatically from the teams that checked in.
- **Search:** Global search finds teams as well as players.

### Improved

- **Matches:** Restart Map now warns that recorded rounds will be set aside.
- **Matches:** Only staff can restore rounds or restart a map from the match page.
- **Tournaments:** the Teams tab shows each team's seed and, before the draw, its projected seed and first opponent.
- **Tournaments:** seed numbers stay with teams across the bracket, standings and match page, and hovering a team highlights its route.
- **Tournaments:** Check-in Review explains when a tournament is held for too few teams and what the organizer can do.
- **Tournaments:** when several tournaments are live, each gets its own tab on the Tournaments page.
- **Tournaments:** during check-in the panel shows how many teams have checked in, with a link to the Teams tab.
- **Tournaments:** deleting a tournament first shows its played matches, demos, clips, rated matches and awards, and asks you to confirm.
- **Tournaments:** AIM Rules is replaced by the Freeze Time and Round Restart Delay settings.
- **Tournaments:** a tournament with too few eligible teams after check-in now waits for its organizers instead of being cancelled, and admins are told.
- **Tournaments:** deleting a tournament first shows its played matches, demos, clips, rated matches and awards, and needs them confirmed.
- **Tournaments:** The bracket's Fit button now switches between fitting the bracket and 100% zoom.
- **Tournaments:** The tournament chat no longer shows a webcam room button.
- **Matchmaking:** DEAFCS Pro starts searching right away, without an extra language popup.
- **Settings:** Webhook addresses are hidden until you press the eye icon.
- **Site:** Times older than an hour show the exact date and time, including in notifications.
- **Matchmaking:** The DEAFCS Pro popup simply says "Only English language".
- **Site:** Opening and closing the left sidebar is smoother, also with the chat open.

### Fixed

- **Teams:** Only the team owner can hand over a team, and the owner can no longer be removed or demoted by others.
- **Profiles:** The match filters on player profiles fit on one line on more screen sizes.
- **Teams:** The Save button in the team editor is fully visible again on every screen size.
- **Matches:** Restoring a round no longer counts kills and damage from the interrupted round twice.
- **Tournaments:** stages always play the tournament's map pool, and match type and map pool lock once the bracket is drawn.
- **Teams:** the Team Owner picker shows the player's flag and name, and Save stays visible in the Team Editor.
- **Tournaments:** a stage with its own settings now keeps the tournament's camera, auto-cancel and server settings, and a refused save no longer saves half of the tournament settings.
- **Tournaments:** the Check-in Review page shows its headings and labels instead of raw text keys.
- **Tournaments:** Check-in Review shows the full date and time each team registered.
- **Tournaments:** Registration counts on tournament cards now show the players actually on each team.
- **Tournaments:** a tournament's map pool now always reaches its stages and matches, and it can't change once the bracket is drawn.
- **Tournaments:** an expired tournament match is no longer always awarded to the first team; it goes against the team that didn't show up, or to an admin.
- **Tournaments:** a tournament that ends before its final has no podium instead of a wrong one, and a final won by a bye gets the right medals.
- **Matches:** a match whose maps all end level now ends as a tie instead of staying live.
- **Tournaments:** The Round Restart Delay organizers choose now applies in matches.
- **Tournaments:** Opening registration for an invite-only tournament no longer notifies every player.
- **Tournaments:** The Teams tab count now matches the teams shown after check-in, and seeds no longer appear twice.
- **Profiles:** Open Twitch Widget goes to the new widget address.
- **Site:** Opening the left sidebar no longer cuts off labels or pushes buttons outside it.

## Patch Notes | November 6, 2026

### New

- **Matchmaking:** DEAFCS Pro is a scheduled solo 5v5 queue with Captain Pick for top-ranked players.

### Improved

- **Tournaments:** Cancelling a tournament or resetting it to Setup now asks for confirmation first.
- **Matchmaking:** Each mode shows its map pool when you hover or tap its info icon.
- **Matchmaking:** The Play page shows Competitive, Wingman and Duel in a cleaner layout on every screen size.
- **Matches:** Maps a Grand Final did not need are muted and marked Not needed.
- **Tournaments:** Administrators can delete a tournament with rated matches without voiding ELO first.
- **Tournaments:** Organizers can reset or change a match winner without voiding ELO first.

### Fixed

- **Matches:** Organizers can cancel a match that already has a demo.
- **Matches:** Canceled matches no longer count toward player statistics or leaderboards.
- **Matches:** A canceled match keeps its played maps, rounds and stats, even before its demo arrives.
- **Tournaments:** Changing an earlier result no longer swaps the teams of a canceled match that was already played.
- **Tournaments:** A tournament can no longer be cancelled or deleted while its matches are being played.
- **Matches:** A match started again right after a cancel keeps its server.
- **Matches:** A canceled match that is started again replays the unfinished map from warmup and keeps its finished maps.
- **Tournaments:** A tournament with played matches can no longer be reset to Setup.
- **Tournaments:** Organizers can recover an unfinished series without awarding a win, and resetting a match keeps its finished maps.
- **Tournaments:** Correcting the winner of a final now finishes the tournament again.
- **Tournaments:** The Grand Final uses its own best-of and map advantage at any bracket size.
- **Matches:** The match page shows the upper bracket team's Grand Final map advantage.
- **Tournaments:** The bracket and match lists show a Grand Final's full series score, including the upper bracket advantage.
- **Matches:** Setting a map winner in a Grand Final counts the upper bracket team's map advantage.

## Patch Notes | November 5, 2026

### New

- **ELO:** Competitive ELO now depends mostly on the result and the strength of both teams.
- **ELO:** Your match rating counts for up to 10% of your Competitive ELO change.

### Improved

- **ELO:** Competitive gains and losses get smaller above 10,000 ELO, about ±50 at 20,000.
- **ELO:** Competitive ELO can't drop below 1,000.
- **Matchmaking:** Leaving a Competitive, Wingman or Duel match now gives 50% of the gain if your team wins, or twice the loss if your team loses.
- **Matchmaking:** In Duel and Wingman, wins against the same opponent are worth less after three meetings in 7 days. Losses aren't affected.
- **ELO:** The ELO popup explains leaver and repeat opponent adjustments.
- **ELO:** Your current ELO and past matches stay as they are. The new rules apply to matches that start from now on.

### New

- **Tournaments:** Organizers can turn on AIM Rules for a Duel tournament: 3 second freeze time, free Kevlar, no buy time and a 3 second delay between rounds.

### Improved

- **Navigation:** The left sidebar has clearer groups, with Community, Updates and Info sections.
- **Navigation:** The Info section stays open or closed after a page reload, like the other sections.
- **Rules:** General, Matchmaking and Tournament Rules are now one Rules page with three tabs.
- **Navigation:** The Info section is shorter, and the collapsed sidebar shows it as one icon with a menu.

## Patch Notes | November 2, 2026

### Fixed

## Patch Notes | November 1, 2026

Team history on profiles and team pages, a new Changelog page, a new search, and matchmaking by region.

### New

- **Teams:** Player profiles now show current and previous teams, with available membership history.
- **Teams:** Team pages show a roster history of current and former members.
- **Teams:** The team activity feed shows when players join, rejoin, leave or are removed.
- **Changelog:** A new Changelog page under Info shows patch notes by day, with search, filters and a dot for new changes.
- **Matchmaking:** EU and SA are now separate region groups, and the Play page counts the queue for your region group.
- **Search:** Press Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on Mac) or use Search in the left menu to find pages, players, FAQ answers and your settings.

### Improved

- **Matchmaking:** Each region group can open or close matchmaking modes on its own, and the Play page shows what is open in your region.
- **Matchmaking:** If a mode closes while you are in its queue, you leave the queue and get a message.
- **Profiles:** Choose EU or SA from a dropdown in settings or on your profile. Staff approve the change.
- **Notifications:** Your notifications, friends and My Matches appear sooner when a page opens.
- **Performance:** The Teams page, league seasons, tournament bracket previews and the utility library stay quick when many people view them at once.
- **Matches:** The scoreboard keeps players in ADR order in every view, rows move smoothly when their positions change, and stat numbers roll like an odometer.
- **Teams:** The team page tabs are one full-width bar under the team header, with the active tab highlighted, and it stays on screen while you scroll.
- **Leaderboards:** K/D, HS% and rating boards filtered by source or role load much faster.
- **Matches:** Report details no longer show an empty profile row.

### Fixed

- **Matches:** Stream and demo startup progress now shows the workshop map download in the right order.
- **Matchmaking:** When a mode is closed, a ready check that fails no longer puts you back in its queue or starts a new one.
- **Highlights:** A clip you make private leaves the public Highlights and Watch lists right away.
- **Teams:** Team cards show a space before "vs" in the Next and Last rows.
- **Accounts:** After your role changes, the site picks up your new permissions right away without a reload.
- **Matches:** A late duplicate status from a game server no longer ends the next map early or loses a map's result.
- **Steam:** Steam messages that Steam delays or rate-limits are retried instead of lost, and are never sent twice.
- **Seasons:** Rebuilding a season's ratings now keeps its tournament matches, so season ELO stays correct.
- **Matches:** Report a Player opens 5 minutes after the match ends, and the match page shows the time left.
- **Matches:** Player names show correctly when choosing who to report.
