# Patch Notes | November 13, 2026

## Improved

- **Leaderboard:** Podium and table numbers roll smoothly when you switch category or filters.
- **Search:** Ctrl+K shows teams first, with an exact tag match at the top.
- **Alerts:** long alert messages wrap on phones and keep their line breaks.
- **System Alerts:** links in alerts open safely, and links to DEAFCS stay in the app.
- **Matches:** Clutch results load faster.
- **Profiles:** A new profile banner shows your ranks for all three modes and every team you play for.
- **Profiles:** Your ELO now sits beside your recent matches, and the full match list has its own Matches tab.
- **Profiles:** A Share button copies the profile link, and the Steam ID copies in one click.

## Fixed

- **Leaderboard:** Large pages (100 players) no longer lag when switching category.
- **Matches:** T and CT stats such as ADR now count every round played on that side, not only rounds with a kill or death.
- **Servers:** Connect on a public server now joins it instead of only opening CS2.
- **Matchmaking:** a party can only queue when every member meets the queue's role requirement.
- **Alerts:** a system alert you dismissed shows again when staff put it back up.
- **Profiles:** Map totals on a profile no longer count cancelled matches.
- **Profiles:** The match list waits to load before saying a player has no matches.
