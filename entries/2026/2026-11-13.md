# Patch Notes | November 13, 2026

## New

- **Leaderboards:** ADR and KPR boards can show T side or CT side only.
- **Teams:** A team's Activity tab lists results, roster changes and awards, with filters.
- **Teams:** The team banner shows the league season the team is playing in.
- **Teams:** Share a team's page with the new Share button.

## Improved

- **Leaderboard:** Podium and table numbers roll smoothly when you switch category or filters.
- **Search:** Ctrl+K shows teams first, with an exact tag match at the top.
- **Alerts:** long alert messages wrap on phones and keep their line breaks.
- **System Alerts:** links in alerts open safely, and links to DEAFCS stay in the app.
- **Matches:** Clutch results load faster.
- **Profiles:** A new profile banner shows your ranks for all three modes and every team you play for.
- **Profiles:** Your ELO now sits beside your recent matches, and the full match list has its own Matches tab.
- **Profiles:** A Share button copies the profile link, and the Steam ID copies in one click.
- **Teams:** The roster lists players in name order, Admins included.
- **Teams:** A team's matches show the score, result and opponent from that team's side.
- **Teams:** The team overview shows the team's recent matches beside the roster.
- **Teams:** The roster is a team photo; tap a player to see their photo, and League and History sit beside it.
- **Profiles:** The profile tabs and match filters are tidier and fit on one row.
- **Profiles:** The profile header shows the player's role and team instead of the rank plates.
- **Profiles:** Country, Steam ID, Steam and Twitch links sit in one tidy bar, with a larger flag.
- **Profiles:** Hover or tap an ELO value to see the next tier and how much ELO it takes.
- **Profiles:** Recent matches are cleaner and show your ELO after each match and the change.
- **Profiles:** The Matches tab shows your ELO after each match beside the gain or loss.
- **Profiles:** Recent matches has a quick DEAFCS/External switch, and match rows show the date and time on the left.

## Fixed

- **Leaderboard:** Large pages (100 players) no longer lag when switching category.
- **Matches:** T and CT stats such as ADR now count every round played on that side, not only rounds with a kill or death.
- **Servers:** Connect on a public server now joins it instead of only opening CS2.
- **Matchmaking:** a party can only queue when every member meets the queue's role requirement.
- **Alerts:** a system alert you dismissed shows again when staff put it back up.
- **Profiles:** Map totals on a profile no longer count cancelled matches.
- **Profiles:** The match list waits to load before saying a player has no matches.
- **Teams:** A Grand Final result in the team activity shows the series score with the upper bracket's map head start.
- **Teams:** Only the team owner, team Admins and members with the Invite role can invite players to a team.
- **Matches:** Clutch results in rounds with kills in the same moment match the original calculation again.
