# Patch Notes | November 23, 2026

## Improved

- **Leaderboard:** An info icon on Player of the Month explains how the winner is chosen.
- **Matches:** Community GOTV waits 30 seconds after the last match's stats are ready before switching to another match.
- **Highlights:** Administrators can set a daily window for rendering highlight clips in App Settings, so live matches and streams keep the GPU. It is off by default.

## Fixed

- **Awards:** Automatic leaver and no-show bans no longer stop a player from becoming Player of the Month.
