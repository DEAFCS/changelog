# Patch Notes | November 21, 2026

## New

- **Profiles:** Your awards and badges now share one Trophy Case, and you can arrange them in the order you like.
- **Profiles:** Your awards and badges now share one Trophy Case, and you can choose the three you feature.
- **Profiles:** Every profile shows its Career Level and XP progress, and you can open your own Career History from the XP bar.

## Improved

- **Matches:** Community GOTV shows a short countdown before a newly started match can be streamed, so broadcasts no longer start with a broken view.
- **Matches:** The Community GOTV control in the server bar is shorter, with details behind an info button.
- **Profiles:** Performance now shows Maps, Arsenal, Opening Duels, Clutches and Consistency as cards that open their full details.
- **Profiles:** Performance matches the new 5Stack layout, with KAST and HS% tier bars and stat tiles that open a match graph.
- **Profiles:** Maps, Opening Duels, Clutches and Arsenal open as compact views inside Performance.
- **Profiles:** Share, Message, Friend and Sanctions now sit in the bottom row of the profile header, next to the Level and XP.
- **Profiles:** A trophy or badge shown in one of your three showcase boxes is no longer repeated in the small icons.
- **Profiles:** Badge tooltips show the Career XP the next Badge level gives.
- **Profiles:** Badge tiers now give more Career XP, up to 310 XP for a full Badge.
- **Profiles:** No Scope, Zeus and Knife Badges give Career XP for 5v5 matches only.

## Fixed

- **Profiles:** Clutch win rates count eliminated-opponent clutch wins; saved clutches remain attempts.
- **Profiles:** The empty 1v1 clutch row is gone from Performance.
